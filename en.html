From the manufacturer to your shelf.

Three steps, and one contact in Switzerland for everything in between.

  1. Selecting manufacturers

    We choose every manufacturer personally. Before we list a product, we require the safety report, the complete INCI list and proof of good manufacturing practice (GMP).

  2. Importing responsibly

    We handle transport and customs clearance. We only list products for which we have all the documents we need as an importer for the Swiss market: product information file, correct labelling and traceability of every batch.

  3. Supplying retailers

    You order from a partner in Switzerland, with invoices in Swiss francs and contacts based in Switzerland.

For retailers

Do you run a drugstore, a perfumery, a concept store or a beauty salon? Send us a short note on what you are looking for, and we will send you our current range and price list.

We are starting with a small number of selected partner shops and take time for each of them.
